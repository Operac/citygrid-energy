Supply Operations Manager

CityGrid Energy Limited is seeking a competent, proactive and results-driven Supply Operations Manager to oversee and coordinate its supply and distribution operations.

The ideal candidate must have strong negotiation skills, excellent numerical abilities and practical experience in logistics, supply chain management or petroleum product distribution.

Location and travel: This role is based in Lagos. Because of the nature of the job, candidates must be open to regular travel, including between our Lagos and Ibadan operations.

Key responsibilities

  • Manage day-to-day supply and distribution operations.
  • Negotiate competitive prices and favourable terms with depots, suppliers and transporters.
  • Coordinate truck dispatch, loading, transportation and product delivery.
  • Monitor stock levels and ensure accurate inventory reconciliation.
  • Supervise drivers, field personnel and other operational teams.
  • Monitor product quantities from loading to final delivery.
  • Liaise with relevant government officials, regulatory agencies and road transport authorities.
  • Identify and resolve supply chain and logistical challenges.
  • Monitor operational costs and ensure efficient resource utilisation.
  • Prepare regular supply, inventory and operational reports.
  • Maintain strong working relationships with suppliers, customers and business partners.

Requirements and qualifications

  • 1–3 years of relevant experience in supply chain management, logistics, operations or petroleum product distribution.
  • Strong negotiation and communication skills.
  • Excellent numerical, analytical and calculation skills.
  • Good knowledge of diesel distribution and petroleum supply operations.
  • Ability to engage professionally with government officials, regulatory agencies and transport authorities.
  • Experience in fleet, driver and logistics management.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.
  • Strong inventory management and reconciliation skills.
  • Excellent leadership and team management abilities.
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making skills.
  • High level of integrity, accountability and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work under pressure and travel when required.

Added advantage

  • Previous experience working with petroleum depots, tanker drivers and industrial fuel customers.
  • Knowledge of petroleum product measurement, truck calibration and delivery procedures.
  • Relevant professional certification in Supply Chain Management or Logistics.
